# Prep Note Format

One note per interview session, in the folder `JOBSEARCH.md` § Workspace
names (default `Interviews/YYYY-MM-DD <Company> <Stage> Prep.md`). Debriefs
after the session go in the application note under `## Interviews`, not
here. Worked example:
`examples/workspace/Interviews/2026-10-28 Proseware Hiring Manager Prep.md`
in the harness repository.

```markdown
---
creation_date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags:
  - job-search
  - interview-prep
status: active
---

# <Company> <Stage> Prep, <Month D, YYYY>

Application: [[<Company> <Short Role> Application]]. Research: [[<Company> Company Research]]. Stories: [[Story Bank]].

## Session

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| When | <Weekday YYYY-MM-DD, HH:MM to HH:MM zone (IANA zone)>. <Calendar event verified date.> |
| Format | <recruiter screen, hiring manager, coding, system design, behavioral, panel; tools such as a shared editor; stated rules such as no AI assistance> |
| People | <names and roles> |
| Join or Location | <"link in the calendar invite", or the address and arrival instructions> |

## Opening

<A spoken introduction of 60 to 90 seconds: level and specialization, current and previous anchors, why this role.>

## Stories

1. **<Story Bank title>** maps to "<JD requirement>". Guide: <the shape of the answer>. Probe to expect: "<follow-up>". Avoid: <the overclaim to steer clear of>.

## Technical Lens

- <Topics to review for this format, with the reasoning to rehearse. Anything not in the record is framed as "I would".>

## Practice Questions

1. "<Likely question>." <Answer guide pointing at a story or the technical lens.>

## Questions To Ask

- <Three to six questions that qualify the role and the process.>

## Logistics And Compensation

- Published band: "<verbatim>".
- <What the user has already said about pay, if anything, and the approved wording if asked. No figure without the user's decision.>
- <Why-leaving answer: the approved line from JOBSEARCH.md § Candidate.>
- <Equipment, travel, timing checks.>

## Checklist

- [ ] <Rehearse the opening aloud, under 90 seconds.>
- [ ] <Rehearse the top two stories at about two minutes each.>
- [ ] <Test the link, camera, and audio, or confirm the route and arrival time.>

## Studio

<Path to the interactive studio if one was generated, or "not generated".>
```

## Rules

- Every story comes from the story bank and every outcome from its approved
  claims. A gap found while preparing becomes a story-bank task, not an
  invented answer.
- Prepare for anything flexed in the submitted materials (see the tailoring
  rules' interview exposure section).
- State every time in the user's time zone with the zone abbreviation; when
  the interviewer is elsewhere, give both.
